## On every plan, including free

- Browser verification (proof-of-work + rolling-key tokens)
- Automation scoring on every request
- Rate limiting
- Monitor and standard enforcement modes
- Evidence log of blocked and challenged requests

Nothing that keeps a live site standing up is behind a paywall. A free user
being scraped is still protected.

## The tiers

| Plan | Price | Domains | Requests/mo | Retention |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 1 | 25,000 | 7 days |
| Solo | $20 | 1 | 250,000 | 30 days |
| Indie | $50 | 3 | 1,000,000 | 90 days |
| Studio | $150 | 10 | 5,000,000 | 1 year |
| Agency | $300 | 25 | 25,000,000 | 3 years |

Paid tiers add strict mode, protected routes, generated terms and the terms
acceptance gate. Indie adds evidence seals and reports. Studio adds agreements
and notice drafting. Agency adds priority support.

## Billing never gates traffic

FounderShield sits in front of your users. Enforcing a request quota would mean
degrading your site to collect our revenue — the same trade the proxy already
refuses when it fails open.

Request volume is **metered and reported, never enforced at the edge**. Going
over produces a conversation, not a throttle. Domain count and feature access
are enforced in the dashboard, where refusing is safe.

<Note>
Request counts are estimates. Ordinary traffic is sampled, which is precisely
why allowances are advisory and nothing is ever throttled.
</Note>

A failed card keeps its plan rather than silently stripping your protection
settings.
