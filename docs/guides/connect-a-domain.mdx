## The two records

| Type | Name | Value |
|---|---|---|
| TXT | `_foundershield.<your-domain>` | `fs-verify=<token from the dashboard>` |
| CNAME | `<your-domain>` | your edge hostname |

The TXT record proves control of the domain. The CNAME routes traffic. Nothing
is enforced until traffic actually reaches the proxy.

## What verification checks

Both of these must pass before a project becomes `active`:

1. **Ownership** — the TXT record is read over DNS-over-HTTPS and compared to
   the project's token.
2. **Reachability** — `https://<domain>/__fs/v1/health` is fetched, and the
   response must identify *this* project.

<Warning>
The second check is not a formality. It is what prevents a domain from being
marked live while its traffic still bypasses the proxy entirely.
</Warning>

## Common failures

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Nothing was returned yet">
DNS propagation. Wait a few minutes and retry. The dashboard reports what it
found instead, which usually makes a typo obvious.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Reaches a proxy, but a different project">
Two projects claim the same hostname, or a stale CNAME points somewhere else.
Check for a duplicate DNS record.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Ownership confirmed, traffic not arriving">
The TXT record resolved but the CNAME has not taken effect. Confirm the CNAME
target matches the edge hostname shown in the dashboard exactly.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## You cannot verify yourself

Domain status, DNS verification and the project secret are all server-side
writes. The dashboard has no path that lets an account assert any of them about
itself. This is deliberate: a customer who can mark their own domain verified
can also fabricate the circumstances of everything recorded under it.
