</Warning>

## Generated terms of use

Clauses describing the access controls your site actually enforces, served at
`/__fs/v1/terms`.

The version **is** a hash of the rendered text. Editing any clause necessarily
bumps the version and re-asks everyone who accepted the old wording. You cannot
quietly alter terms someone already agreed to — which is the usual way clickwrap
gets thrown out.

A matching machine-readable policy is published at
`/.well-known/ai-permissions.json`, because the terms assert it exists.

## NDAs

Trade-secret protection is conditional on having taken reasonable measures to
keep information secret. An NDA with everyone who sees it is the canonical one.

The detail worth knowing: US law requires these agreements to carry a
whistleblower-immunity notice, and omitting it forfeits exemplary damages and
attorney's fees against that person. Most templates omit it. This one includes
it, defaults it on for US governing law, and flags it if you turn it off.

Signing records assent — who, when, from where, and a hash of the exact text. A
signed agreement is frozen at the database level: editing it raises an error,
because a signature against a document that no longer exists is worse than no
signature.

<Note>
This records assent. It is not a qualified electronic signature service.
</Note>

## Draft notices

Two different documents, because the underlying facts differ:

- **DMCA takedown** — built from republication facts, and refused unless every
  statutory element is present. Software cannot hold a good-faith belief, so the
  sworn statements start unticked and there is no way to default them on.
- **Circumvention / access demand** — built from the evidence log. Each draft
  reads its own evidence and says so when it is weak.

Nothing is ever sent. There is a button to record that *you* sent something.
There is no send button, and there will not be one.
