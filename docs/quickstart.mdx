---
title: "Quickstart"
description: "From signup to a protected domain, in monitor mode."
---

<Steps>
<Step title="Create a project">
Sign in, then add the domain you want to protect and the origin it should
forward to. The origin must be a different hostname from the protected domain,
or the proxy would forward to itself.
</Step>

<Step title="Add two DNS records">
The dashboard shows both. A `TXT` record proves you control the domain; a
`CNAME` routes traffic through the proxy.

| Type | Name | Value |
|---|---|---|
| TXT | `_foundershield.example.com` | `fs-verify=<your token>` |
| CNAME | `example.com` | your edge hostname |
</Step>

<Step title="Verify">
Verification checks the TXT record over DNS-over-HTTPS **and** fetches
`https://<domain>/__fs/v1/health` to confirm traffic actually reaches the proxy
and is serving the right project. Both must pass.

<Warning>
A domain only becomes active when both checks pass. You cannot mark your own
domain verified — that flip is a server-side write, deliberately outside your
account's reach.
</Warning>
</Step>

<Step title="Stay in monitor mode for a week">
`monitor` records exactly what enforcement *would* have done and blocks nothing.
Read the activity log daily. If anything you recognise as a real visitor would
have been blocked, do not move on.
</Step>

<Step title="Switch to standard">
Once the log is clean, move to `standard`. Check your own site in a private
window, on mobile data, and with an ad blocker enabled.
</Step>
</Steps>

## What to watch first

False positives, by a distance. A blocked customer is worse than a hundred
admitted scrapers, because you will hear about it from a support ticket rather
than a dashboard.
